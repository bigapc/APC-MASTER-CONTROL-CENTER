# SafeWorkConnect — Product Blueprint v0.1

**Parent:** Armstrong Pack Company (APC)

## Working identity

**SafeWorkConnect** is the current working product name. **CommunitySafeWorkConnect** remains a possible expanded/community-facing brand option pending naming and trademark review.

SafeWorkConnect is an AI-assisted local work and services connection platform designed to connect people, skills, experience, businesses, organizations, and local opportunities.

## Product principle

SafeWorkConnect is not defined as a simple job board.

Core connection loop:

**Skills → Opportunity → Match → Communication → Work → Outcome → Reputation → Local Business Growth**

## User groups

### Workers and service providers
- Skilled trades
- Construction workers
- Auto mechanics and repair professionals
- Lawn care and snow removal providers
- Assembly and general labor
- Pre-employment/job-seeking individuals
- Retired workers seeking flexible work
- Vocationally trained workers
- Degree holders and experienced professionals
- Lawyers and legal-aid professionals, subject to appropriate licensing/authorization requirements
- Event planners
- Local organizers
- Consultants and other service professionals

### Opportunity posters
- Homeowners and residential clients
- Local businesses
- Commercial businesses
- Contractors
- Property owners/managers
- Organizations
- Event organizers

## Core profiles

Provider profiles may include:

- Skills and trades
- Previous employment/work experience
- Education and degrees
- Vocational training
- Certifications where applicable
- Service radius
- Desired hourly rate
- Fixed-price preferences
- Availability
- Portfolio/work examples where appropriate
- Verification/status information where legally and operationally appropriate

## Opportunity posting

A client or business may post:

- Job or service description
- Location
- Desired date/time
- Required skills
- Pay offered
- Hourly wage or fixed-price budget
- Desired pay range where applicable
- Urgency
- Residential or commercial classification
- Project category

Initial categories include:

- Construction projects
- Auto mechanic and auto repair
- Assembly
- Lawn care
- Snow removal
- Skilled trades
- General labor
- Professional services
- Legal and legal-aid discovery/referral, subject to jurisdiction and professional rules
- Event services
- Local/community organizing

## Local matching

The product should support location-aware opportunity discovery, including configurable distance/radius preferences such as 5, 10, or 25 miles where supported by the user's location and platform settings.

Matching should consider authorized inputs such as:

- Skills match
- Location/radius
- Availability
- Pay expectations
- Experience
- Certifications or qualifications where relevant
- Opportunity requirements

## AI and virtual assistance

AI is a decision-support and coordination layer, not an autonomous employer, legal professional, or decision-maker.

Planned capabilities may include:

- Profile and opportunity intake assistance
- Skill-to-opportunity matching assistance
- Clarifying questions for job posters and providers
- Communication preparation
- Suggested match explanations
- Scheduling assistance
- Phone/video connection coordination through approved integrations
- Owner/business relationship support
- Follow-up and workflow assistance

The virtual assistant must disclose its automated role where appropriate and respect authorization, privacy, and applicable professional/legal boundaries.

## Business relationship and monetization direction

A primary APC objective is to build relationships with local and commercial businesses and create measurable value by helping connect them with relevant workers and service providers.

Potential models requiring separate business/legal review include:

- Business subscriptions
- Paid opportunity postings
- Featured listings
- Local business relationship packages
- Recruitment/service connection fees where lawful and appropriate
- Sponsored local service categories
- Premium matching and workflow tools

No final fee structure is approved by this blueprint.

## Differentiation target

The uniqueness goal should come from the integrated APC operating model rather than unsupported claims that no competitor has similar features.

Potential differentiating system concepts for evaluation:

1. **Opportunity Radius Engine** — skills, pay, availability, urgency, and local distance considered together.
2. **APC Work Connection Assistant** — guided intake and communication coordination for both sides.
3. **Skill-to-Service Passport** — portable profile combining work history, skills, training, and availability with user-controlled sharing.
4. **Local Business Connection Network** — tools focused on building repeat relationships between APC, local businesses, and service providers.
5. **Work Opportunity Lifecycle** — structured tracking from request through match, communication, completion, and outcome.

Any name, software method, slogan, logo, trademark, or patent strategy requires separate availability and legal/IP review before protection claims are made.

## APC architecture position

SafeWorkConnect should remain a distinct APC product/application.

It may later connect to the APC Master Control Center through authorized, governed integrations for high-level operational and platform health monitoring. It should not be merged into SafeConnect or the safety products.

Shared enterprise capabilities may include approved patterns for:

- Authentication
- Security
- Notifications
- Maps/location infrastructure
- AI gateway interfaces
- Audit logging
- Observability
- APC branding/legal footer

Product-specific work matching, profiles, opportunities, business relationships, and professional-service rules remain within SafeWorkConnect.

## Safety, privacy, and professional boundaries

The product must not represent an AI assistant as a lawyer, licensed professional, employer, or emergency authority. Professional credentials, background checks, verification, payments, employment classification, marketplace rules, and data/privacy requirements require separate implementation and legal review.

## Build principle

> **Measure twice. Build once.**

Do not start a duplicate repository or large implementation until existing APC repositories are audited for reusable components and the MVP scope is confirmed.
